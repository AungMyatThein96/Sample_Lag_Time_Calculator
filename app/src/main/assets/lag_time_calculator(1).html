Well Site Lag Time Calculator

Mudlogging / wellsite geology tool; Bit-to-Surface cuttings lag time. (A.M.Thein_Geol)

Units

Field: depths in ft, diameters in inches, volume in bbl. Metric: depths in m, diameters in mm, volume in liters.

Well & Hole Geometry

Well has a second open hole section (liner / directional hole-size change)
Open Hole 1 runs from the casing shoe to the crossover depth. Open Hole 2 runs from the crossover depth to total depth, at the smaller diameter.

Drill String

Include drill collars / BHA (in current open hole section)

Pump Data

Calculate output from liner size (triplex)
Single-acting pump: output/stroke = (π/4) × liner² × stroke length × number of cylinders × efficiency, converted to the selected volume unit. Default is 3 for a standard triplex pump.
